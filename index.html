Privacy

Your data stays on your device

Vault is a single HTML file with no backend. Entries are saved in your browser's local database (IndexedDB) — there's nothing to sign up for, nothing uploaded, and no analytics watching you.

Exports are yours to protect. Backups and share files can be encrypted with a passphrase you choose at export time (AES-256-GCM, PBKDF2 key derivation).

  • No accounts, no cloud sync, no tracking
  • Open the page once, then it works offline
  • Backup reminders when you've made changes
Straight talk: the vault on your device isn't encrypted at rest, so it's only as safe as the device and browser profile it lives on. Lose a passphrase and there's no recovery — keep it somewhere safe.
Get it on your device

Install in seconds

No app store. Open Vault in your browser and add it to your device.

🍎 iPhone & iPad

Open Vault in Safari, tap the Share button, then choose Add to Home Screen.

🤖 Android

Open Vault in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.

💻 Desktop

In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon in the address bar to launch Vault in its own window.
